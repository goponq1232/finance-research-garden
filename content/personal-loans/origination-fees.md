# How Origination Fees Change What You Receive

The amount shown on a loan agreement and the amount deposited into your bank account may differ.

Suppose:

- Stated loan amount: **$10,000**
- Origination fee: **5%**
- Fee: **$500**

Depending on how the lender structures the loan, you could receive **$9,500** rather than the full $10,000.

## Compare three separate figures

1. **Gross loan amount**
2. **Fees**
3. **Net proceeds**

If an expense requires exactly $10,000, a deducted fee can create an immediate shortfall.

Fees also matter when comparing [[apr-vs-interest-rate|APR and interest rates]].
