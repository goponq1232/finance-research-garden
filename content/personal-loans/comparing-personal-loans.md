# How to Compare Personal Loan Offers

A personal-loan offer can look attractive because of a single number: a low interest rate, a small monthly payment, or a large approval amount.

A better comparison looks at the **whole structure**.

## Seven numbers and terms to compare

1. **Loan amount**
2. **Interest rate**
3. **APR**
4. **Origination and other fees**
5. **Monthly payment**
6. **Repayment term**
7. **Total scheduled repayment**

Also check the amount that will actually reach your bank account.

## Why the monthly payment can mislead

A lower monthly payment may simply mean the debt is spread across more months.

For example, the same balance repaid over 60 months can feel easier each month than a 24-month loan, while potentially keeping you in debt longer and increasing total borrowing costs.

## Compare the offer inside your budget

A lender decides whether it will approve you. Your budget decides whether the payment is comfortable.

Stress-test the payment against:

- an unexpectedly expensive month,
- a temporary income reduction,
- a car or home repair,
- higher utility costs.
