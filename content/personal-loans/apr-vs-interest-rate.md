# APR vs. Interest Rate

The **interest rate** represents the basic cost charged for borrowing money.

The **annual percentage rate (APR)** can provide a broader comparison because certain finance charges may be reflected in it.

## A simple example

| Feature | Loan A | Loan B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest rate | 9% | 10% |
| Origination fee | 5% | 0% |
| Term | 36 months | 36 months |

If you look only at the interest rate, Loan A appears cheaper.

Once fees are considered, the difference may be less obvious.

That is why a careful comparison should consider both the advertised rate and the APR, then verify the total repayment and net proceeds.
