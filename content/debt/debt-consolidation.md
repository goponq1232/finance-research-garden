# Debt Consolidation: What to Compare

Debt consolidation can turn several payments into one. That can make repayment easier to manage, but **simplification is not automatically savings**.

## Compare the before and after

Before consolidating, write down:

- current balances,
- current APRs,
- current monthly payments,
- expected payoff periods.

Then compare the proposed consolidation loan:

- APR,
- origination fee,
- new monthly payment,
- term,
- total scheduled repayment.

## The behavioral risk

If credit-card balances are paid off with a personal loan and the cards are then used heavily again, you can end up with **both** the consolidation loan and new card balances.

A consolidation strategy works best when the new debt structure is paired with a realistic budget.
