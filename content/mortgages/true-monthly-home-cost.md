# The True Monthly Cost of a Mortgage

The principal-and-interest payment shown by a mortgage calculator is only part of the potential monthly cost of homeownership.

Depending on the loan and property, also consider:

- property taxes,
- homeowners insurance,
- mortgage insurance,
- HOA fees,
- maintenance,
- repairs.

## Rate versus total housing cost

A slightly lower mortgage rate can be attractive, but upfront closing costs and the expected time you will keep the loan can affect whether the offer is actually better.

Mortgage affordability should therefore include both the financing cost and the recurring cost of owning the property.
